What this search is

It looks for your words in the “Indications and usage” section of FDA drug labels. “Migraines” also finds “migraine”. It counts a label when the words are there. The one thing it skips is what follows “not indicated”, “not recommended” or “not intended” in a sentence, so a drug does not show up for a condition its label rules out.

So a result is a mention, not always a treatment. Search and the first results are sunscreens, because their labels say “decreases the risk of skin cancer”.

Synonyms are not matched either. and give different lists.

Not medical advice.
