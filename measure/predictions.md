## Queries (Python `time.perf_counter()` around execute and fetch, warm, mean of 3)

## What would surprise us (and what we would check)

- `parse` at 100% more than 20% slower than at 25%: then the kept rows cost more than we think (check the loader's memory and the `detail` counts).
- COPY growing more than linearly: then the TOAST writes or the WAL, not the stemming, dominate (check `db` CPU in `docker stats`: below 100% would mean waiting on disk).
- The query without the index not using parallel workers at 50% and 100% (read the saved EXPLAIN plans): then P12 and P14 will be about 2× higher.
- The db memory peak much higher than 300 MiB: then the cgroup counts the page cache of everything written, and the number says more about Linux than about PostgreSQL.
